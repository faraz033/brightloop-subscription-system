1. DRAFT — Initial Subscription Composition

Draft Mode

When an order is created, it begins in Draft. The administrator or sales rep sets the customer, selects a recurring plan (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), and attaches products with optional variants and custom quantities. No billing commitments are active yet.

Stage Rules & Operational Behavior
  • ✓ Editable line items, quantities, and pricing variants
  • ✓ Configurable payment terms (Immediate, Net 15, Net 30)
  • ✓ Can transition to QUOTATION when ready for customer review

Zero Duplication

Let Recurring Billing Follow the Rhythm.

Brightloop's recurring invoice engine automatically evaluates active subscriptions against due periods while preventing double billing through strict mathematical uniqueness.

The Period Uniqueness Guarantee

Preventing duplicate recurring runs automatically

Enforced by Database

When the invoice generator runs, it calculates each subscription's elapsed period start date and period end date. If an invoice has already been created for that exact subscription and period window, the generator skips it cleanly. Running the generator twice produces 0 duplicate records.

Database Constraint: UNIQUE(subscription_id, period_start) ensures that even simultaneous batch triggers cannot create redundant invoices.

Everything you need to know about Brightloop's capabilities, subscription logic, and billing operations.

Brightloop is a full-featured subscription management and recurring billing platform. It centralizes product catalogs, pricing variants, quotation proposals, customer contracts, automated recurring invoicing, tax and discount engines, payment ledgering, and financial telemetry in one intelligent system.
Brightloop is built for three primary audiences: Administrators (who govern products, plans, taxes, discounts, and staff), Staff Members (who manage daily customer orders, quotations, and invoice payments), and Customers (who access their own self-service portal to view contracts and invoices).
A subscription binds a customer to a recurring plan and one or more product line items with quantities and variants. Subscriptions progress through structured lifecycle stages (Draft ➜ Quotation ➜ Confirmed ➜ Active ➜ Closed), ensuring orderly contract execution.

Brightloop natively supports four recurring billing frequencies: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly. The automated invoice generator calculates elapsed periods precisely according to the selected frequency.
Yes. Products support multi-tier variants (e.g., storage capacities, user seat tiers, or service levels) with customizable surcharge price deltas attached to the base product price.
A quotation is an early stage of a subscription record. Once the customer reviews the proposal and terms, staff or administrators update its status to "Confirmed" and then "Active", which unlocks recurring billing generation.
Invoices can be generated automatically in batch runs via the recurring billing engine. The engine inspects active subscriptions, determines due billing windows, applies applicable tax and discount rules, and records draft or confirmed invoices.
Yes. Brightloop computes itemized discounts (fixed amount or percentage based on eligibility and quantity) and applies configured regional tax rates (such as standard 18% GST) directly into invoice totals.
Yes. Payments can be recorded in partial installments or full settlements via Bank Transfer, UPI, Credit Card, or Cash. The system tracks remaining balances and marks the invoice "Paid" once the full amount is satisfied.

Administrators possess complete system governance: creating products, configuring plans, setting tax rates, defining discount rules, onboarding staff accounts, and reviewing live financial reports.
