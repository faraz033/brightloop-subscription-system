Every Subscription Has a Journey. We Keep It Moving.

Brightloop guides recurring agreements across strict, predictable lifecycle stages. Click any stage below to inspect its operational rules and transition constraints.

1. DRAFT — Initial Subscription Composition

Draft Mode

When an order is created, it begins in Draft. The administrator or sales rep sets the customer, selects a recurring plan (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), and attaches products with optional variants and custom quantities. No billing commitments are active yet.

Stage Rules & Operational Behavior
  • ✓ Editable line items, quantities, and pricing variants
  • ✓ Configurable payment terms (Immediate, Net 15, Net 30)
  • ✓ Can transition to QUOTATION when ready for customer review

Zero Duplication

Let Recurring Billing Follow the Rhythm.

Brightloop's recurring invoice engine automatically evaluates active subscriptions against due periods while preventing double billing through strict mathematical uniqueness.

⚡

The Period Uniqueness Guarantee

Preventing duplicate recurring runs automatically

Enforced by Database

When the invoice generator runs, it calculates each subscription's elapsed period start date and period end date. If an invoice has already been created for that exact subscription and period window, the generator skips it cleanly. Running the generator twice produces 0 duplicate records.

Database Constraint: UNIQUE(subscription_id, period_start) ensures that even simultaneous batch triggers cannot create redundant invoices.
